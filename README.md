# Bilen — Cybersecurity & Threat Detection

Bilen is a cybersecurity and threat-detection platform designed to identify,
analyze, and respond to digital security threats.

It focuses on monitoring suspicious activities, detecting anomalies,
identifying potential attacks, analyzing security events, and providing
actionable intelligence for organizations and infrastructure. Bilen supports
defensive cybersecurity by helping organizations understand their digital
environments and respond to emerging threats.

**Core vision:** Build intelligent cybersecurity infrastructure capable of
protecting Ethiopia's increasingly digital systems.

Part of [Project ARWE](https://github.com/henokakriso).
